Namma Pass: shared peak-hour commutes

A prototype by Muhammed Sirfan C for Namma Yatri. Simulated riders travel from five Bengaluru areas to a tech park on the Outer Ring Road between 7:00 and 10:00. The engine groups them, caps every rider's extra travel time, and splits the fare. The driver collects every seat fare, about 1.4 times a solo trip, and there is no commission.

Corridor map (simulated, not to scale)

Coloured dots are grouped riders. Grey rings are riders who could not be placed. The yellow star is the tech park.

Groups formed

    Service runs only in the peak windows, 7:00 to 10:00 and 15:00 to 18:00; drivers take normal rides the rest of the day. Assumptions: 18 km/h peak speed, 1.3 road factor, 3 minutes wait per stop, solo fare = ₹30 + ₹8 per road km (to be replaced with the live fare engine), seat price = 1.4 ÷ riders × solo fare (35% in a group of 4, 47% in a group of 3), the driver collects every seat fare, driver subscription ₹25 a day for autos and ₹90 for cabs (public reports, to be replaced with live values), 22 days and 44 trips a month. 3 and 6-month plans lock the fare rather than discount it. All riders are synthetic. This is an independent prototype, not an official Namma Yatri product.
